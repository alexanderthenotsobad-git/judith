About

I am a software developer and technical leader focused on AWS based APIs and enterprise integrations. In my current role, I work with large-scale systems including SAP, Salesforce, Power Automate, Snowflake, and internal applications, and I serve as the primary owner for our team's Apache Kafka infrastructure.

In addition to development, I act as a technical partner to our team's architect and engineering leadership. I support architectural decision-making, contribute to system design discussions, and lead knowledge-sharing sessions. I enjoy mentoring and helping newer developers and interns.

I am committed to community and representation in tech. Through my involvement with AISES (American Indian Science and Engineering Society), I support Native American students and early-career professionals by sharing my experiences and helping create pathways into technology careers.
